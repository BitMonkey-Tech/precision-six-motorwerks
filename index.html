ABOUT

Southwest Florida's Porsche dealer alternative.

Precision Six Motorwerks was founded in Fort Myers on a simple idea: give Porsche owners in Southwest Florida a knowledgeable, transparent, highly specialized alternative to the dealership, without losing the personal relationship that comes with an independent shop.

FOUNDER

Joseph “Joe” Russo

Joe is a lifelong Porsche enthusiast with a decade of experience working at Porsche dealerships in SWFL. He worked in all facets of the industry from sales to financing and management.

An avid track enthusiast, husband, and father of three, Joe envisioned more than a repair shop. He wanted to create a welcoming place where Porsche owners could connect, share their passion, and feel confident in the care their cars receive. Joe saw a need in our community for a Porsche only facility that offers a premier dealer alternative.

PORSCHE CERTIFIED GOLD TECHNICIAN

Brendan

Brendan brings nearly a decade of hands on Porsche experience to Precision Six Motorwerks in Fort Myers, backed by extensive factory training, certifications, and a genuine passion for the marque. He achieved Porsche Certified Gold Technician status, representing the highest level of technical training, diagnostic ability, and experience within the brand.

Over the course of his career, he has worked extensively with Porsche vehicles ranging from classic air cooled models to the latest generations of high performance road cars. His experience includes advanced diagnostics, mechanical repair, drivetrain and transmission work, and performance focused service.

Beyond the dealership environment, Brendan has remained closely involved with the Porsche and motorsports community throughout Southwest Florida. His involvement with local Porsche enthusiasts, motorsports events, and track focused vehicles has given him the opportunity to work with owners who care just as much about their cars as he does.

For Brendan, Porsche has never simply been a brand he works on. It has been a major part of his life. From learning to drive a manual transmission in a 993 Turbo to building a career around the cars, Precision Six Motorwerks is the next chapter of that passion.
