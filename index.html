ABOUT

Southwest Florida's Porsche dealer alternative.

Precision Six Motorwerks was founded in Fort Myers on a simple idea: give Porsche owners in Southwest Florida a knowledgeable, transparent, highly specialized alternative to the dealership, without losing the personal relationship that comes with an independent shop.

Owned and run by Joe Russo, the shop operates as a one bay, appointment only operation by design, so every car gets full attention instead of being one of a dozen jobs moving through the bay at once.

FOUNDER

Joe Russo

Joseph John Russo founded Precision Six Motorwerks with a clear mission: to give Porsche owners in Naples, Fort Myers, and across Southwest Florida dealer level technical expertise paired with an enthusiast's commitment to performance.

Joe's passion for Porsche engineering was forged over years at official Porsche dealership service departments in both Naples and Fort Myers, FL. That factory trained background gave him a deep, hands on understanding of the marque, from classic air cooled flat six engines to modern GT platforms and today's high tech drivetrains, expertise he now brings to independent Porsche owners as a dedicated Porsche repair and maintenance specialist.

For Joe, Porsches aren't just vehicles to service. They're built to be driven. As an active figure in several car clubs around Southwest Florida, Porsche owner groups, and high performance driving events, Joseph regularly takes cars to the track to push limits, dial in suspensions, and refine setup dynamics. That real track experience directly informs his work at Precision Six Motorwerks, giving Naples and Fort Myers area clients an edge whether they need precise factory scheduled maintenance, custom Porsche performance tuning, or dedicated track prep.

"Precision Six Motorwerks was born out of a passion for the engineering behind every flat six. We treat every client's car as if it's heading straight to the grid."
Joseph John Russo
PORSCHE CERTIFIED GOLD TECHNICIAN

Brendan

Brendan brings nearly a decade of hands on Porsche experience to Precision Six Motorwerks in Fort Myers, backed by extensive factory training, certifications, and a genuine passion for the marque. He achieved Porsche Certified Gold Technician status, representing the highest level of technical training, diagnostic ability, and experience within the brand.

Over the course of his career, he has worked extensively with Porsche vehicles ranging from classic air cooled models to the latest generations of high performance road cars. His experience includes advanced diagnostics, mechanical repair, drivetrain and transmission work, and performance focused service.

Beyond the dealership environment, Brendan has remained closely involved with the Porsche and motorsports community throughout Southwest Florida. His involvement with local Porsche enthusiasts, motorsports events, and track focused vehicles has given him the opportunity to work with owners who care just as much about their cars as he does.

For Brendan, Porsche has never simply been a brand he works on. It has been a major part of his life. From learning to drive a manual transmission in a 993 Turbo to building a career around the cars, Precision Six Motorwerks is the next chapter of that passion.
