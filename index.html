COMMON QUESTIONS

Will using an independent shop void my factory warranty?

No — the federal Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act protects your right to use an independent shop for maintenance and repairs without voiding your factory warranty, as long as the work meets factory standards. That's exactly how we operate.

Are your Porsche techs factory trained and certified?

Yes — our technicians are factory trained and certified, so your car gets the same expertise as the dealer without the dealer overhead.

Do you use OEM parts?

Yes — genuine OEM parts on every service and repair, which is also what keeps your factory warranty intact if it's still active.

Do you do factory-scheduled maintenance?

Every interval on the factory schedule — from routine oil services to the major milestones — done with genuine OEM parts, and a service history you can hand to the next owner.

Do you do performance upgrades and tuning?

Yes — suspension tuning, ECU calibration, and exhaust upgrades, dialed in for everything from a mild street car to GT3 and GT4 clubsport builds.

Can you mount and balance new tires?

Yes. We mount and balance on Hunter's Porsche-approved equipment, so ride quality and handling stay dialed in with new rubber.

Can you diagnose and repair a check-engine light?

Yes — factory-level diagnostics and root-cause repair across your water-cooled Porsche's so you're not paying to replace parts that were never the problem.
